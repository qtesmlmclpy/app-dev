# Shaolin Soccer (少林足球)

**Shaolin Soccer** is a legendary 2001 sports comedy film that perfectly merges *Shaolin Kung Fu* physics with competitive soccer. 

## Movie Specifications

| Metric | Details |
| ----------- | ----------- |
| **Director** | Stephen Chow |
| **Box Office** | $42.8 Million |
| **Run Time** | 113 minutes (Original Cut) |
| **Country** | Hong Kong |

## The Team & Special Skills
- **Sing ("Mighty Steel Leg"):** The main protagonist who uses insanely powerful kicks.
- **Hooking Leg:** A master of balance and redirection.
- **Iron Head:** Can smash or redirect soccer balls using his incredibly durable skull.
- **Empty Hand:** Uses lightning-fast goalkeeper reflexes inspired by Tai Chi.
- **Iron Shirt:** Capable of absorbing impact and trapping the ball using his body.
- **Light Weight Vest:** Defies gravity to glide and jump exceptionally high.

## Iconic Storyline
> "If a person has no dreams, they are no different from a salted fish." 
> — *Sing*

The narrative follows a group of fractured, down-on-their-luck Shaolin brothers who have lost their way in modern society. They reunite under a crippled former soccer star to unleash their ancient martial arts skills onto the football field, taking on the sinister, technologically enhanced **Team Evil** in the ultimate showdown.

## Production Trivia
* **World Record:** It was the highest-grossing film in Hong Kong box office history at the time of its release.
* **Special Effects:** It required over 400 digitally altered shots to create the iconic comic-book-style action.
* **Video Game Influence:** Many action sequences were stylistically inspired by classic fighting video games like *Captain Tsubasa* and *Street Fighter*.
